About

Jari Laru

Municipal politician · university lecturer · researcher · entrepreneur · father · food enthusiast · road tripper

I am an educator from Oulu, Finland, working across several roles: researching technology-enhanced learning, teaching future teachers at the University of Oulu, serving in local decision-making as a city council member, and delivering training through my company Larux. Outside work, family life, cooking, and long road trips across Europe bring balance and energy.

Jari Laru – university lecturer, researcher, municipal politician

Four roles

My professional life is built around four complementary roles.

City Council Member

I have been active in local politics throughout the 2000s. Today I serve in the Oulu City Council and in the Education and Culture Committee, with focus on education, culture, and urban development.

Read more

University Lecturer

Doctor of Education (2012). University lecturer in technology-enhanced learning at the University of Oulu. Current focus includes teachers' AI literacy in the Generation AI project.

Read more

Trainer & Speaker

Through Larux, I provide professional learning and keynote talks for educators. Core themes: AI in education, digital pedagogy, and technology-enhanced learning.

Read more

Researcher

My publications are listed in Research.fi and ORCID (0000-0003-0347-0182). Research areas include mobile learning, collaborative learning, and AI literacy.

Read more

From Oulu, living in Jaali

I was born in Oulu and have lived here all my life. Our family – my spouse and three children – lives in a detached house in Jaali, in the Oulu area. Jaali is calm and close to nature, while still connected to city services.

For me, Oulu is more than a hometown – it is also a field for research, politics, and community action. I have been involved in local decision-making and civic initiatives for years.

As a council member, I work especially for education, culture, and local democracy.
