Politics

Jari Laru, politician

Decision-making in municipal and regional governance is built on cooperation. No individual councillor moves things forward without networks, dialogue, and shared direction.

I have been involved in civic influence from an early age. My background in residents' association work, municipal politics, and education is reflected in the way I do politics: practically, evidence-based, and with a long-term perspective.

Motions Speeches Election history Affiliations

Municipal and regional politics for cooperation, education, and a functional everyday life.

Experience and roles

City Councillor

I serve on the Oulu City Council and the Education & Culture Committee.

Long experience

I have been involved in municipal decision-making and positions of trust across several electoral terms.

Research background

An academic background helps in understanding large wholes and the consequences of decisions.

Education focus

Education, culture, and sport are the foundation of wellbeing and the core of the city's vitality.

Long-term goals

Functional everyday life nearby

Safe schools, working infrastructure, and smooth basic services in every district.

Open governance

Decision-making must be transparent, understandable, and easily accessible to residents.

Balanced urban structure

The city is developed with the strengths of different areas in mind, not with a focus only on the city centre.
